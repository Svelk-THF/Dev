Every week, the Taranaki Air Ambulance makes, an average of 7 flights carrying patients to life-changing or life-saving medical care not available in Taranaki. The Taranaki Air Ambulance Trust (TAAT), a not for profit trust, was established in 2006 to provide a quality air ambulance service for the people of Taranaki.
