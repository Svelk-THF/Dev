Wharepuni marae is located approximately 10 km north of Hawera. Its principal hapū include Ngāti Tānewai and Ngāti Tūpaea of the iwi Ngāti Ruanui. The marae connects ancestrally to the waka Aotea and the maunga Taranaki.
